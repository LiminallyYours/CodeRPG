---
name: On the coast road
chance: 2 in 6
entries:
  - A gull drops out of the wind, snatches at your coin purse and flaps off with a coin. (effect: coins -1)
  - A shepherd's dog trots alongside you for a while, then vanishes into the gorse. (effect: none)
  - Loose stones skid under your boots at the cliff edge and you go down hard. (effect: hurt 1d2)
  - In the ruts of the road lies a small corked bottle, dropped by some traveller. (effect: gain healing_tonic) (once)
---
Things that can happen on the clifftop road. The rules script rolls for them when the player arrives.
