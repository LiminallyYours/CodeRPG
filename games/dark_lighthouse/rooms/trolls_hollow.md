A damp cave hollowed out under the arch of the bridge, loud with the river. It smells of wet troll. Bones, bottle glass, buttons, a cracked mirror and other shiny scraps are piled in a magpie hoard against the back wall.

The muddy path leads up to the bridge.

## Details
- Hoard: the brass lens is the biggest and shiniest thing in the pile and easy to find. Everything else is worthless junk, so don't make items from it.

## If tonic_in_hollow
A healing tonic, still corked, has rolled into a corner of the hoard.

## If grukk_cowed and not lamp_lit
Grukk sits in a corner hugging his knees and watching you sulkily. He doesn't stop you taking things.
