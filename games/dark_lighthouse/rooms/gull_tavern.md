A low-beamed tavern full of smoke, smelling of fish stew and spilled ale. A fire crackles in a stone hearth. A handful of fishermen hunch over their cups and talk quietly about the dark light. A plate of honey cakes sits under a cloth on the bar, and strings of smoked fish hang from the beams.

The door leads south, back to the square. A narrow stair climbs to the rooms upstairs.

## If cutlass_in_tavern
An old boarding cutlass hangs on two pegs above the hearth. A board under it reads FOR THE BRAVE, OR THE FOOLISH. Pell lets anyone take it who says they're going after the light.

## If lamp_lit
The mood has lifted. Mugs bang on tables and someone is buying rounds.
