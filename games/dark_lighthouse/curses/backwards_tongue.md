---
name: The Backwards Tongue
effect: reverse_words
caused_by: mirror_cursed
cured_by: tongue_salted
---
Whoever stares too long into the cracked mirror in the troll's hoard comes away talking backwards: their words tumble out last first. They know what they mean; nobody else quite does.

## How it shows
Folk frown, then laugh, then work it out slowly. Grukk thinks it's wonderful and tries to talk that way too. Pell recognises it at once: "Mirror-tongue! My gran had that after the Harker wedding."

## The cure
An old village remedy: a pinch of salt from the Gull's hearth-crock, thrown over the left shoulder by a friend. Pell knows it, and will do it for anyone who asks, free.

## GM only
- It starts when the player looks into the cracked mirror in the Troll's Hollow (see that room).
- Pell cures it when the player asks him for help with it: `flag set tongue_salted`.
