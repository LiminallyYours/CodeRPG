---
name: Around the station
chance: 1 in 6
entries:
  - The lights flicker, die, and come back a shade dimmer than before. (effect: none)
  - A seam in the ceiling bursts and sprays you with icy seawater; you slip and bang your knee on a bench. (effect: hurt 1d2)
  - In the pocket of a soggy jacket on a hook, you find a credit chip someone forgot. (effect: coins +1) (once)
  - Far below, metal groans against metal as the swell shifts the stilts. (effect: none)
---
Things that can happen inside the station. The rules script rolls for them when the player arrives in a room that links this table.
