After a few minutes in the cracked comms headset, with feedback squealing in one ear, the player can't stop signing off like a radio operator: everything they say ends with "…over." It isn't magic, just a very sticky habit.

## How it shows
Bucket logs it as a fault ("Beep. VISITOR: VERBAL LOOP DETECTED"). Dr. Halloran finds it unbearable.

## The cure
Someone has to snap them out of it. Dr. Halloran will, the first time she hears it: a sharp snap of her fingers in their face and a "Stop. That."

## GM only
- It starts when the player puts on the comms headset in the Control Room and tries to call out (see that room).
- Halloran breaks it the first time she hears it: `flag set habit_broken`.
